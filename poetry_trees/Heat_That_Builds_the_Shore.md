### Core Insight
The poem does not try to redeem time.  
It consents to remain inside the motion that both makes and unmakes the foam.  
Sweat builds the shore.  
The heat is in the tools.  
Victory is light carried on the shoulders.

---

### Central Association Chain (⊛)

Sea of yore  
→ flotsam of what was kept and discarded  
→ brief shimmer / flickering path  
→ lingering tides  
→ a promise kept *in* the water  
→ foam as the briefest architecture the sea allows  
→ the sensors that must be dulled against the constant itch  
→ empty houses standing while bodies sleep on concrete  
→ the long conflict with unfiltered reality  
→ the quiet decision to remain inside the motion rather than escape it
